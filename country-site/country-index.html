Ukraine
Україна

As one of the largest and most populated countries in all of Europe, Ukraine shares a rich history as one of the oldest Slavic traditions dating back to the Kievan Rus'. Centered on the historic city of Kyiv, this pioneering Eastern Slavic state contributed in shaping the political, cultural and religious foundations of the surrounding region that is still seen today. Despite centuries of foreign rule and conflicts, Ukraine stands as a bastion for it's inherent culture, traditions, and spirit of independence that it continues fighting to preserve to this very day.
